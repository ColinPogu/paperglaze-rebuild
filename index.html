Welcome

A steady hand, twenty years in the making.

Shannan Inman has been in the calligraphy business for twenty years. Every piece that leaves the studio is drawn by hand with a pointed pen, which means no two are ever quite the same.

From the first ink test to the final flourish, the goal is simple. Lettering that feels personal, looks timeless, and suits the moment it was made for.

Start a Project

One of a kind, every time.

Pointed pen calligraphy is slow work on purpose. The pressure of the nib, the angle of the hand, and the choice of ink all shape a style that cannot be copied and pasted.

That is what makes a Paperglaze suite yours alone, whether it is a wedding invitation, a welcome sign, or a single framed verse.

Bella Scriptura

Scripture prints, drawn by hand

The Bella Scriptura collection holds more than fifty different Old and New Testament verses, each hand drawn with pointed pen and ink, then printed on fine art, natural, acid free paper.

Prints are black and white and come mostly in 4 by 6 and 5 by 7, with a few 8 by 10 sizes. They are sold through the Paperglaze Etsy shop.

Learn Calligraphy

Find your own hand

Paperglaze has been teaching calligraphy since 2014, to hundreds of students across seven different states. Classes are warm, patient, and built for real beginners as much as for the already hooked.

Private home parties are also available, a relaxed way to gather friends around pen, ink, and a glass of something nice.
